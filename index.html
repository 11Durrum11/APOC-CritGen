🔫 Guns — Sorcery & Steam misfire roller & firearms reference

In Sorcery & Steam, a firearm attack that rolls a natural 1 may misfire: roll 1d20 on the Misfire table (p.146). Masterwork weapon: −1. Percussion cap ammunition: −1. APOC house rule: perpetual Fog-of-Life damp — +2 unless the powder is kept dry (sealed cartridges, a dry load keeper, etc.), DM's call.

Rules — crits, fumbles, definitions

Critical hits (original CritGen rules): when a crit is confirmed, draw a number of cards equal to the crit multiplier − 1 (×2 = 1, ×3 = 2, ×4 = 3). If a result can't be applied, the default is ×2 damage as normal. For GMs: these effects can be deadly against players — the original recommends using crit effects against players only from major villains/NPCs, or requiring a feat to use the system.

Critical fumbles: fumbles have no codified rules; the original gives three methods — (1, recommended) on a natural 1, make a confirmation roll with full BAB + modifiers; if it would miss, you fumble; (2) same, but with the first roll's modifiers; (3) any natural 1 is a fumble. Optional: Weapon Focus lets you draw 2 fumble cards and pick one (Greater Weapon Focus: 3); a player may bank a crit (roll normal damage, no effect) to negate a later fumble in the same encounter; a PC can't fumble more than once per combat; non-proficient attackers threaten a fumble on a natural 1 or 2.

Bleed: bleed damage is dealt at the end of the affected character's turn. Unless noted, ability bleed is damage, not drain. Bleed ends with any magical cure spell or a DC 15 Heal check as a standard action.
Save DC: unless a DC is listed, it equals the confirmation roll used to score the critical hit (after all modifiers). Saves affect only the additional critical effects, never the base weapon damage. Put your confirmation roll in the box at the top and it is stamped on every card.
Until healed: always refers to the additional damage or bleed, not the weapon (or spell) damage.

APOC Survival mode (house, this app): turns on the Fog of Life theme; flags plane/teleport effects as Severed (planar travel is cut off and extra-dimensional magic is dead in APOC canon) with a replacement effect; adds a Raw Magic Blight roll to Magic crits and fumbles (raw magic blights in APOC); and enables the Fog-damp misfire modifier. Everything APOC is labelled in the app and is off when the toggle is off — with the toggle off this is a straight D&D 3.5 generator.
